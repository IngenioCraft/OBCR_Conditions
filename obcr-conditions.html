How to read this: the colored banner up top is the quick answer — whether it's calm enough to row now and whether conditions are building or easing over the next few hours. Wind and gusts are what actually decide rowability in the bay.

About wave height: Beekman Beach sits inside a sheltered bay, so wave models can't resolve local chop and often read near zero — treat it as an open-water estimate only and trust wind/gusts instead.

These are automated readings to help planning, not a substitute for a coach's or rower's judgment on the day.
